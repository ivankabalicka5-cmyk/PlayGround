## Moonberry Marsh

A complete, single-level jelly platform adventure inspired by bouncy buddy games. Gather three moonberries, dodge marsh moths, and reach the waystone.

- **Move:** Arrow keys or A / D
- **Hop:** Space or Up / W
- **New mechanic — Lumen Pulse:** Press E to reveal ghost platforms and briefly stun nearby moths. It recharges automatically.
- **Touch:** On-screen move, hop, and pulse buttons appear on smaller screens.

Open `index.html` directly, or serve this folder with `python3 -m http.server 3002` and visit `http://localhost:3002`.
